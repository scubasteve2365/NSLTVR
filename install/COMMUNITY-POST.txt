**NSLTVR 1.0: New Super Lucky's Tale in VR, on the game's own Lucky's Tale VR camera (free mod, OpenXR)**

New Super Lucky's Tale grew out of Lucky's Tale VR and still carries that game's VR camera. This mod switches it back on and renders both eyes through OpenXR. Every level becomes a toy world around you, with Lucky running right in front of you. True native stereo: both eyes from the same game moment, no alternate-eye rendering, no warping. Seated, with a gamepad.

**What it does**
- Native stereo with the game's own per-level VR camera from Lucky's Tale VR
- Normal game speed at any headset rate (72, 90, 120 Hz)
- HUD, pause book, dialogue, costume shop, title and loading screens in the headset
- In-headset menu in the game's style: hold both sticks, or press Insert
- HUD placement, resolution, click sounds, and one-press "VR baseline" and "best VR performance" settings
- If VR does not start, a message on your monitor says why
- One bat file installs and uninstalls. No game files are changed

**What you need**
- New Super Lucky's Tale on Steam
- A PC VR headset with OpenXR: SteamVR, or Virtual Desktop's VDXR
- A gamepad. **DualSense:** turn on Steam Input for the game

**Please read**
- Tested on Pimax Dream Air (SteamVR, 72 Hz) and Quest 3 (Virtual Desktop VDXR, 90 Hz). Other setups should work; reports welcome, with BepInEx\LogOutput.log from the game folder.
- The right stick does not turn the camera; it is the game's own VR camera.
- The mod is not signed, so Windows may warn you. The README covers it.
